Electronegativity (Pauling)

How strongly an atom attracts electrons

Electronegativity measures an atom's tendency to attract shared electrons in a chemical bond.

Across a period

Generally increases left to right. More protons pull electrons more strongly in the same shell.

Down a group

Generally decreases going down. Outer electrons are farther from the nucleus and more shielded.

Tip: Noble gases are often shown as N/A because they rarely form bonds.
